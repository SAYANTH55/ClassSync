# Research Limitations Log

A living register of known limitations. Each entry is either **open** (being
mitigated) or **accepted** (documented in the dissertation's Limitations
section). Entries are never deleted — resolved items are marked resolved, so
the research trail stays auditable.

| ID | Limitation | Cause | Impact | Mitigation | Status |
|----|------------|-------|--------|------------|--------|
| L1 | All current images come from a single capture session (studio, 2026-03-09): identical lighting, background, clothing, frontal pose. | Dataset originated as formal portrait shoot, not as a training-data collection exercise. | No intra-class variation; a model trained on this alone cannot generalise to classroom conditions (domain shift). | Multi-session smartphone-video collection starting 2026-07-20; DSLR set retained as enrollment gallery / controlled test set. | **Open** |
| L2 | ~1 image per student — insufficient for from-scratch CNN training. No within-class train/val/test split is possible; augmenting one image across splits would constitute data leakage. | Same as L1. | CNN training (Phase 7) is blocked until more data exists. All data-independent phases (preprocessing, architecture, pipeline, documentation) proceed now. | Data-collection protocol v1: ≥2 videos × 15 s per student, different rooms/lighting, ±30° head pose; ~3 fps frame extraction → 60–90 images/student. | **Open** |
| L3 | Identity labels are provisional (auto-assigned S01–S56 in filename order); contact-sheet verification by the researcher not yet performed. | Labeling requires personal knowledge of the cohort. | A duplicate person under two IDs would corrupt class counts and, later, evaluation. | Verify each row of `data/labels/labels.csv` against `reports/dataset_inspection/contact_sheet.jpg`; set `verified=yes`; rerun `organize_dataset.py build`. | **Open** |
| L4 | Face-localization method not yet fixed: whether the classical Viola–Jones (Haar cascade) detector counts as a "pre-trained model" under the supervisor's constraint is unconfirmed. | Constraint wording covers pre-trained *models*; Haar cascades are 2001-era boosted classifiers shipped with OpenCV, not deep networks — but they *are* trained artifacts. | Determines Phase 3 design: use OpenCV Haar cascade vs. implement/train our own detector from scratch. | **Resolved 2026-07-18: supervisor ruled that ALL pre-trained detectors are prohibited (incl. | L6 | Phone-session frames will be face-localized semi-automatically (trained detector) rather than fully by hand, introducing possible annotation noise in recognition training crops. | Exhaustive manual annotation of thousands of video frames is infeasible within the project timeline. | Mislocalized crops could add label-geometry noise to CNN training data. | Stratified per-student sample annotated manually; geometry source recorded per crop in the manifest; detector outputs spot-checked via QA sheets; detector evaluation restricted to human-annotated geometry only. | **Open (by design)** |
